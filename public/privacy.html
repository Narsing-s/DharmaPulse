ॐ DharmaPulse Privacy

DharmaPulse is designed to work without an account. Favorites, language, theme, and reminder preferences are stored locally in your browser or Chrome extension.

This release does not require a paid API or a server account for devotional activity.

Browser capabilities such as notifications, speech synthesis, sharing, clipboard access and PWA installation are controlled by your browser/device permissions.

Festival dates are reference content and may vary by region, tradition and local panchang.
